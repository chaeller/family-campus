# Curricula of our areas

Where tiles belong: a short summary of the official curricula of the areas
in `campus.json`, read by the skills `fc-ingest` and `fc-improve`. This is
an **invented example** – replace it with the official documents of your
country and keep downloaded PDFs in `material/` (never committed).

## carpenter – Carpenter (3 years)

- **Source:** the official training plan for carpenters of your country
  (link, version, date checked) and the curriculum of the vocational school.
- **Levels:** `y1`–`y3` are the three regular years of the training.
- **Topics per year** and the subjects used for them in this folder:

| Year | Drawing and planning | Materials and production | Applied maths |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `y1` | Types of drawings, freehand sketches, simple joints | Working safely, solid wood, hand tools | Converting units, lengths and areas |
| `y2` | Workshop drawings, lists of materials, fittings | Wood-based panels, glues, machining | Areas and volumes, angles |
| `y3` | Complex drawings, interior fittings, design | Surface treatment, CNC basics, wood moisture | Costing basics |
